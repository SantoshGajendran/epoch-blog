Reciprocal Rank Fusion (RRF) combines ranked lists from disparate retrieval strategies without requiring score normalization. The smoothing constant `k=60` balances top-ranked high-confidence matches against longer-tail semantic relevance, preventing sparse keyword outliers from dominating vector semantic proximity.
