Speculative decoding relies on a lightweight draft model proposing tokens verified in parallel by the target model. If `num_speculative_tokens` is too high, unaccepted draft tokens waste verification compute. A draft length between 4 and 6 tokens typically yields the best speedup across standard text generation benchmarks.
